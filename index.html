How to Play

The wall has a hole cut in the shape of one view of the block — its front view, top view, or side view.

  • Front view — looking straight at the block.
  • Top view — looking straight down from above.
  • Side view — looking at it from the left or right.

Use the rotate buttons to turn the block 90° at a time. Watch the X-Ray panel — it shows you exactly which parts line up (green), which parts are still missing (amber), and which parts would smash into the wall (red).

When every square is green, hit Push Through!

Tip: drag anywhere in the 3D scene to look at the block from a different angle — it doesn't count as a move.
