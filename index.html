How to Play

The wall has a hole cut in the shape of one view of the block — its front view, top view, or side view. On Medium and Hard, two or three walls test different views AT THE SAME TIME, so your rotation has to satisfy all of them at once.

  • Front view — looking straight at the block.
  • Top view — looking straight down from above.
  • Side view — looking at it from the left or right.

Use the rotate buttons — or on a touchscreen, swipe with one finger right on the block (swipe left/right spins it one way, up/down spins it the other way). Watch the X-Ray panel(s) — green means that part lines up, amber means it's still open, red means it would smash into the wall.

When every panel is all green, hit Test Fit!

Tip: drag with two fingers (or the mouse) to look around the scene from a different angle — that never counts as a move.
